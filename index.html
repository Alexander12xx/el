About Elwana Blooms

Grown in Kenya.
Prepared for the world.

Elwana Blooms connects the beauty of Kenyan-grown flowers with buyers and floral markets around the world. We work directly with our own growing partners rather than through brokers, so quality stays consistent from the first cut to the final carton.

From cultivation and harvesting to grading, packing and export, every stage is approached with attention to freshness, consistency and presentation.

  • Direct farm relationshipsWe buy from growers we visit, with agreed varieties and quality benchmarks.
  • Specification-led gradingStem length, bud openness and head size graded to each buyer's brief.
  • Cold chain from harvestHydration, conditioning and cool-store handling protect vase life in transit.

Our origin

From Kenyan soil
to global markets.

Kenya's highland climate gives long daylight hours, cool nights and consistent growing conditions. Elwana Blooms works with farms around Lake Naivasha and the surrounding highlands, where roses and hypericum are grown for export all year round.

  • 1,800m altitude
  • Year-round production
  • Flight time to Europe: ~8h

Our approach

Growing with purpose.

We publish what we can evidence. Where a practice is still being measured, we say so rather than overstate it.

01

Responsible growing

IPM-led pest management, drip irrigation and monitored water use across our partner farms.

  • Reduced chemical load
  • Water recycling at pack houses
  • Soil health tracked annually
02

People

Fair terms, safe working conditions and training that keeps skilled growers on the farms.

  • Written supply agreements
  • Harvest handled in cool hours
  • Skills programmes for farm staff
03

Traceability

Lot-level records that follow a carton from the field it was cut in to the market it lands in.

  • Lot codes on every box
  • Phytosanitary documentation
  • Temperature logged in transit
