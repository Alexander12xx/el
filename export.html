Logistics

How a shipment is handled

Lots from several farms are brought together at our Nairobi facility, where the packing list and lot codes are reconciled against the order. This is also where mixed-carton orders are assembled, so a single delivery can carry more than one variety without confusion at the receiving end.

The consignment is inspected and the phytosanitary certificate issued before loading. The certificate travels with the airway bill, and a copy is sent to you in advance so your broker can clear the shipment on arrival without delay.

Flight times to Europe and the Gulf are around five to eight hours, which is well inside the safe transit window for cut roses. Cartons are loaded into conditioned holds and temperature is logged through the journey. Any temperature excursion is recorded and reported rather than passed on quietly.

On arrival you receive the packing list, airway bill, phytosanitary certificate, lot codes and the quality record for the shipment. If anything in the consignment does not meet the agreed specification, the lot code lets us trace it back to the field and the grading session it passed through.
