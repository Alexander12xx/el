About Elwana Blooms

The people behind the flowers

Elwana Blooms works directly with a small group of highland farms in Kenya, buying on written terms and preparing every order to the buyer's own specification.

1,800m Altitude
Who we are

A short chain between the farm and your market.

Kenya sits close to the equator but its flower farms sit high in the highlands, where days stay long and nights stay cool. That combination produces long stems, strong colour and a vase life that holds up to long-distance air freight.

Elwana Blooms was set up to shorten the route between those farms and the buyers who want them. We buy directly, we specify the quality ourselves, and we prepare each shipment to the standard the buyer asked for — rather than passing the order along and hoping.

  • We buy direct No brokers between the farm and your order, so feedback travels back to the grower.
  • We specify quality Stem length, caliper, bud stage and head size agreed in writing before cutting.
  • We stay accountable Lot codes travel with every carton, so any issue can be traced and answered.

The land

Highland light, cool nights.

Our partner farms sit between 1,700 and 2,000 metres above sea level. The altitude slows growth, which is exactly what gives roses their long stems and lets a grower manage colour and firmness precisely.

  • 1,700–2,000m
  • Drip irrigation
  • IPM-led pest control
