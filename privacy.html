Privacy notice

How Elwana Blooms collects, uses and protects the information you send us through this website.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Applies to: elwanablooms.com and the pages on this site

Draft notice

This notice is written for the current website build and should be reviewed by whoever holds responsibility for data protection at Elwana Blooms before the site goes live, and again if a contact form is connected to a real mailbox or CRM.

1. What we collect

If you submit an enquiry we collect the name, company, email address, telephone number, destination market, expected volume and the description of your requirements that you type into the form. We do not collect payment details through this website.

2. Why we collect it

We use enquiry details for one purpose: to answer you. That means checking availability, preparing a quotation, and following up about the order you asked about. We do not sell your details and we do not pass them to unrelated third parties.

3. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and to maintain a record of supply arrangements with the farms. Records linked to a live commercial relationship are kept for the period required by Kenyan tax and customs record-keeping rules.

4. Who else sees it

Enquiries are visible to the Elwana Blooms export team. Where an order proceeds, operational details are shared with the relevant farm, the consolidation facility and the freight forwarder, because they are required to cut, pack and ship the consignment. We do not share your contact details for marketing purposes with anyone.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. Fonts and mapping assets are loaded from third-party services, and those providers may log technical information such as your IP address as a normal part of serving the file. If analytics are added in future, this notice will be updated before they are enabled.

6. Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Write to elwanablooms@gmail.com and we will respond.

7. Security

The site is served over encrypted connections. Access to enquiry records is limited to staff who need it to answer you, and no system is perfectly secure — if you believe your details have been misused, contact us straight away.

8. Changes to this notice

If this notice changes we will update the date at the top of the page. Material changes affecting existing enquiries will be communicated directly rather than left for you to find.
